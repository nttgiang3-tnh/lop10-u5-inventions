GRAMMAR

💡 Grammar — Expressing Benefits

🤝 allow / enable / help + O + to-V

Use these verbs to say what an invention makes possible for people:

• Vacuum cleaners allow us to clean the house quickly.

• AI enables drivers to find the best routes.

• Smartphones help students to learn languages at home.

🛠️ be used for… / be useful for…

• be used for + V-ing / be used + to-V: Smartphones are used for learning languages. · AquaPlant is used to water the plants.

• be useful for + V-ing / for sb + to-V: E-readers are useful for storing books. · This laptop is useful for students to do homework online.

🌟 Doing sth is one of the (many) benefits of… — put a gerund phrase at the start:
• AI helps drivers to find the best routes. → Helping drivers to find the best routes is one of the benefits of AI in transport.
• Vacuum cleaners allow us to clean quickly. → Vacuum cleaners are useful for cleaning the house quickly.
• Telephones are useful in communicating over long distances. → Telephones are used for communicating over long distances.

STAGE 3 · READING

📖 Reading — The Technology Fair

Read the story, then answer the questions.

It was a sunny Saturday morning, and the School Technology Fair was held in the big hall of Green Star High School. Every team was busy setting up its stall. At their stall, Mai and Tuan introduced their invention, AquaPlant, a smart watering device for plants. “This is AquaPlant,” Mai said. “Its sensor checks if the soil is dry, and then it waters the plants automatically.” The judges came and asked questions, and Tuan explained the benefits: “AquaPlant helps teachers and students to save water and time. It enables the garden to stay green even during the holidays.” Then a young student tried the device on a very dry plant. AquaPlant watered it at once, and everyone clapped happily.

During the break, Mai and Tuan visited a computer shop near the school to choose a laptop for the Inventors' Club. “Can you please recommend a good laptop to us?” Mai asked. The shop assistant showed them the ComLite and the ComMax. The ComLite weighs only 1.5 kilograms and has a 2.4 gigahertz processor, 16 gigabytes of RAM and 512 gigabytes of storage. The ComMax is more powerful, but it weighs 2.6 kilograms and costs seventeen million dong. “Let's take the ComLite,” Tuan said. “It is light and has a large storage space.” The assistant smiled: “Feel free to ask me if you need further information.”

At the end of the fair, the results were announced. Mai and Tuan's team won the prize for the Most Useful Invention, and everyone took a group photo together. Holding the prize, Mai said happily: “Inventions help us to make life better!”

STAGE 3 · SPEAKING

🗣️ Speaking — At the computer shop: making requests

Work in pairs. Role-play a conversation at a computer shop. Student A is the customer who wants to buy a laptop; Student B is the shop assistant. Use the request expressions below, and talk about the processor, RAM, storage space, weight and price.

Making requests: Can you please recommend…? · Can you let me know about…? · Can you tell me (more) about…? · Please let me know (more) about… · I want / would like to know about…
Responding to requests: Sure. · Certainly. · No problem. · What can I do for you? · How can I help you? · Feel free to ask me if you need further information.

Mai (customer): Hello. I'm looking for a laptop for our Inventors' Club. Can you please recommend a good one to me?
Tuan (shop assistant): Certainly. Can you let me know about your needs first?
Mai: Sure. We need a light laptop with a large storage space because we carry it to school every day.
Tuan: Then I think the ComLite is the best one for you. It weighs only 1.5 kilograms, and it has a fast processor and 512 gigabytes of storage.
Mai: Can you tell me more about the price?
Tuan: It costs eleven million five hundred thousand dong.
Mai: Great, we'll take it. Thank you!
Tuan: Feel free to ask me if you need further information.

STAGE 3 · WRITING

✍️ Writing — The benefits of an invention

Write a paragraph (120–150 words) about the benefits of AquaPlant — or another invention you choose. Follow the outline: introduce the invention → give two or three benefits with explanations or examples → conclude with its importance. Use at least TWO benefit expressions from the Grammar section.

Outline: First sentence: What is the invention? · Supporting sentences: What is the first benefit? The second benefit? The third benefit? Give an explanation and/or an example for each. · Last sentence: How important / useful is the invention in our life?

Useful expressions: … is one of the (many) benefits of… · … enable(s) / allow(s) / help(s) us to do sth · … is / are used for doing sth / to do sth · … is / are useful for sth / for doing sth · Firstly… Secondly… Finally… · For example,… · In short,…

AquaPlant is a smart watering device that our team invented for the school garden. This invention brings us several important benefits. Firstly, AquaPlant helps teachers and students to save time. Its sensor checks the soil carefully, and the device waters the plants automatically, so nobody has to water them by hand every day. Secondly, it enables us to save water because it only waters the plants when the soil is really dry. For example, during the dry season, AquaPlant watered our vegetables twice a day and they grew very well. Finally, AquaPlant is useful for keeping the garden green during the long holidays, when no one is at school. In short, AquaPlant is a valuable invention that makes our school greener and our lives easier.
